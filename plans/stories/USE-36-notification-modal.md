# USE-36: A Notification modal

As an app user, I want a dedicated Notification modal on the rail,
so that what happened (and what the app is doing) is reviewable --
not only a transient toast.

**Status**: planned
**Version**: v0.18.0

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A rail-slot modal surfaces notifications (what happened, what
      the app is doing)
- [ ] A lens, never a second inbox (ADR-014: modals are lenses not
      queues)

## Notes

UX-audit finding; pairs with the transparency principle (USE-37).
