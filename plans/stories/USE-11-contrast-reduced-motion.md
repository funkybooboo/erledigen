# USE-11: Contrast and reduced motion respected

As an app user with low vision or motion sensitivity, I want AA
contrast in both themes and full reduced-motion support, so that the
app never excludes me.

**Status**: planned
**Version**: v0.12.0

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] 4.5:1 contrast (normal text), 3:1 (large text), both themes
- [ ] prefers-reduced-motion disables/minimizes all animation
      (shared criteria with USE-6)

## Notes

Ties to USE-6 (animations) and the OKLCH token system.
