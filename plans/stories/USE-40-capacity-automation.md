# USE-40: The 2-day rule and capacity

As an app user, I want overdue routines to rise in priority, and a
daily capacity limit that automation respects, so that the app helps
me keep pace instead of burying me.

**Status**: planned
**Version**: v2.5.0

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] 2-day rule: priority rises over time for routines not completed
- [ ] Daily task limit; distributions respect it
- [ ] Conditional tasks ("complete X to unlock Y")

## Notes

Smart scheduling stays CLI-only (no AI in the UI, ADR-014 + ADR-021).
